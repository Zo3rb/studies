Three main considerations here:

1. When first booting the Arista vEOS VM (but also during subsequent boot processes) please be patient and wait for a couple of minutes for the switch to go through its boot process. Although some warning messages may appear during boot, don't worry about them and keep waiting for the login screen to appear.

2. If, during the boot process, you see multiple messages related to the ZeroTouch feature, then wait for the process to finish, log in to the switch and, after entering the CLI, use the following command to disable ZeroTouch and get rid of those messages:

zerotouch disable

Please note that after entering this command the switch will reboot once again.

3. If more than 10 minutes go by and the switch hasn't finished its boot process, then please consider turning of the virtual machine, going to Settings - System (in VirtualBox) and increasing the amount of RAM to 2048MB or higher. Then turn the VM back on.
