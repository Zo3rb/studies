Once you finish 100% of the lectures inside this course, you can ask Udemy to send you your own Certificate of Completion. IMPORTANT: You cannot request a Certificate of Completion without having all lectures marked as completed.


All you have to do is:

1. Go to your Course Dashboard and click on the trophy symbol, as described in the screenshot below.


2. A pop-up opens up. Here, just click on Get Certificate, as shown below.


3. Next, a new browser tab/window will open up and you will be able to use the Download button to download your Certificate in either JPG or PDF format. Check out the screenshot below.


That's it! I hope this has been useful!
