Otherwise, if this is your first time using Discord, then please follow the steps below to join the server.


1. Go to https://discordapp.com/ and click on Open Discord in your browser.


2. Enter an username, check the Terms of Service checkbox and press the blue button with a white arrow.

Very important! Please try to use the same name on this Discord server as the one you have on Udemy. For instance, if your Udemy name is John Smith, use the same name as a nickname on Discord.


3. On the next screen press Skip.


4. Enter your e-mail address and create a password to claim your Discord account.


5. Check your e-mail and click on the confirmation link inside the e-mail.


6. Once your e-mail has been verified, click on Continue to Discord.


7. Click on the plus sign surrounded by a dotted circle.


8. Click on Join a server.


9. Enter this link https://discord.gg/W4bVWSw inside the text box, then click Join.


10. Welcome to my Discord server! Please read the rules in the #rules-must-read channel!


Please don't share this invite link with people outside this course community. This is our own secret group!
