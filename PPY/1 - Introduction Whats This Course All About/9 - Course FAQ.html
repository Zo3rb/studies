4. The videos are blurry. How can I change the video resolution?

You can open a video and go to the lower right corner of the video, click on the Settings (wheel) button and then pick the desired video resolution. Try different resolutions and see which one is best for you. However, please keep in mind that, in case your Internet connection doesn't support 1080p, Udemy's adaptive video player will choose another resolution for you, using the Auto feature. However, you can try selecting 720p instead. This behavior is Udemy's choice (https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229603888-Adjust-Video-Quality) and I, as a content provider on the platform, have nothing to do with any bad video resolution you may experience. I've uploaded all the videos in the course in 1080p Full HD resolution, so I did my part. Udemy's player and your Internet connection speed are not under my control. However, if you're still not satisfied with the video streaming quality, you can definitely open a support ticket to Udemy (https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us), provide as many details as possible about your issue and their team will be happy to assist.


5. How can I enable the English closed captions (CC) / subtitles and edit the way they are displayed on the screen?

You can open a video and go to the lower right corner of the video, click on the CC button and select English. Captions are disabled by default. Also, you can edit CC settings like the font size using the Caption Settings sub-menu. Please keep in mind that at this point, you can only use Udemy's auto-generated captions, which may contain quite a few errors. However, rest assured that I am currently working on fixing and customizing these captions and improving your learning experience, but it takes a bit of time. I will let you know via Udemy's mailing system when the CCs will be 100% fixed.

7. I'm having issues playing a video / using the Udemy mobile app / loading a lecture / downloading a resource. Who to contact?

Here are some things to look at to help troubleshoot:

  • Please try to clear your cache and cookies Be sure to restart your browser before trying again

  • If you're still experiencing this issue, can you try refreshing your browser?

  • Can you try updating your browser to the latest version? Please try clicking here to see what your browser is. This site will also provides useful information, like whether your JavaScript is enabled, etc. - sending a screenshot of this would be very helpful!

  • To narrow down the issue, could you try accessing the site from a different browser? You can download Google Chrome for free here.

  • Do you know of any extensions or ad blockers you have running on your browser?

  • Have you tried accessing the site with an incognito window? This article will show you how: http://www.wikihow.com/Activate-Incognito-Mode

  • Do you know of any firewalls running on your computer or network?

If none of these suggestions help to resolve the problem, then shoot an e-mail at support@udemy.com telling the team your browser version, your OS, and the exact title of the course you are having trouble with. Please also specify which of the above you have tried so they can try to diagnose this further.

Solving technical issues related to Udemy's platform is beyond of my reach. I'm just the content provider here and all I can help you with is issues related to the course content itself (Python concepts, syntax, errors, applications). Thanks for understanding!
